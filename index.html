About

Prompt. Personal. Professional.

TPT Black Car (Tab’s Professional Transportation) is a privately owned black car transportation company located in Aiken, SC servicing the CSRA and surrounding areas (within 275 miles). TPT Black Car provides transportation to: Airport, Corporate Functions, Special Events, Weddings, Funerals & Personal Needs.
